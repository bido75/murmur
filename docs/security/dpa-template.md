# Data Processing Agreement — outline

Have counsel turn this outline into the final agreement.

1. **Parties and roles.** Customer = controller; Murmur = processor (GDPR/UK GDPR), service
   provider (CCPA/CPRA).
2. **Subject matter.** Recording, transcription and summarization of conversations the customer's
   users choose to record.
3. **Data subjects.** Customer's users; people who speak in recordings; people named in them.
4. **Categories of data.** Voice recordings, transcripts, names, contact details mentioned, work
   content. Special categories only if the customer chooses to record them (see hipaa.md).
5. **Processor obligations.** Process only on documented instructions; confidentiality of staff;
   security measures (Annex II = overview.md + access-control.md); assist with data-subject
   requests (export and deletion tools exist in the product); deletion or return at the end of the
   contract (account deletion, export); make information available for audits.
6. **Subprocessors.** General authorization with notice of changes (Annex III = subprocessors.md);
   30-day objection window.
7. **International transfers.** EU SCCs (2021/914) Module 2/3 and UK Addendum where needed;
   transfer impact assessment.
8. **Breach notification.** Without undue delay, target 48 hours after becoming aware.
9. **Recording consent.** The customer is responsible for lawful basis and for notifying or
   obtaining consent from participants; the product provides consent prompts, state-law warnings
   and consent links to help.
10. **Annexes.** I — processing details; II — technical and organizational measures; III —
    subprocessors.
